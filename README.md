# STARSHIP edf
Hi! this is my project, starship EDF!
 
this is just the planning phase of the project, as i do not have even remotely close to enough funds to build it.
 
# what it is 
essentially, this is a miniaturized version, of space x's STARSHIP rocket, at approximately 1:90 scale. 
this miniaturized starship, as you may be able to tell from the title, is not powered by a rocket engine-and instead powered by one
ELECTRIC DUCTED FAN (EDF) and it will use the 4 onboard flaperons/fins to control itself through free-fall,
and 4 servo-controlled fins under the edf, to essentially "thrust vector" the exhaust gas.

# how will it work?
i cannot promise that it will work, but if it does this is how its gonna happen:
i will use a drone flight computer, with gps, accelerometer, and all that jazz, paired with a crap tonne of custom software,
and some very expensive 2-3cm precision GPS towers and antennas, to make the starship take off from the mechzilla tower (more on that later)
fly up to legal drone limit (120 meters) execute a belly flop maneuver, use the fins to stabilize itself in fall, and then use the edf with its thrust vector control 
to flip itself upright, and be caught with the chopstick arms. i know. very cool.

# is it legal?
i have taken many precautions to make sure that a mini scale rocket can be flown legally. here are the things i need to do once it is built for it to legaly fly:

**1** it is YEARS above the micro drone limit of 250 grams, and it is estimated to be more like 800 grams (almost a full kilogram) and therefore, 
CASA law here in Australia says that it has to be registered, and flown in safe areas at least 10 kilometers away from any air zone AND it has to have
its 11 digit serial number inscribed on the side.

**2** it is an AUTONOMOUS DRONE with its own flight computer and autonomous software, so there are a few laws that apply here:
firstly, it has to remain in my line of site AT ALL TIMES. due to its size, at 120 meters i will still be able to see it, so this isnt an issue.
then, there are the safety laws: i have to have 2 ABORT SWITCHES. 1: the abort switch: if somthing goes horribly wrong, i can flick this switch, and the entire drone shuts down. no motor, no nothing. the 2nd one, is the automatic abort switch: if the drone loses radio contact, for more than one second, it will have to drop whatever manouever it is doing, and immediatly head back to where it took of in attempt to regain signal. if within 40 seconds ti does not regain signal, it will cut all power and fall to the ground.

# COST
if you have seen the BOM, then you will know that this drone costs by itself, 2.8 grand. and i am here to justify it's price:
THE real starship, has gps chips. but, they require signal strength, not accuracy. starship when caught can account for 2-3 meters of error. 
my model, at 1:90 scale has about 2-3 CENTIMETERS of error. GPS is not good at precision. regular gps has an error of about 50cm to about 50 METERS. 
the gps towers, and chips, and antennas, and etc account for 64% OF THE TOTAL COST OF THIS PROJECT. i have considered cheaper alternatives, such as raspberry pi AI camera detection, but i ruled them all out because when the starship is falling through the sky at 50 kilometers an hour, the video is blurry and very fast. AI cannot work with the same speed as a computer, and this is why the project is so expensive: the GPS.

# MECHZILLA
i wont lie to you. that 2.8 grand? isnt even the full cost of this project. this is just the cost of the drone. the MECHZILLA tower, has its own GPS equipment and tower. the starship cannot work without the ground gps equipment, and therefore needs mechzilla to fly. i have split the project into mechzilla and starship to reduce the cost of each project.
